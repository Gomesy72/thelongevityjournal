3. Consult Healthcare Professionals

Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before:

  • Starting any supplement regimen
  • Making significant dietary changes
  • Beginning exercise programs (especially if you have health conditions)
  • Discontinuing prescribed medications
  • Using fasting or caloric restriction protocols
  • Trying biohacking interventions mentioned in articles

4. Supplement Safety

Articles may discuss supplements such as NMN, NR, rapamycin, spermidine, and others. Important warnings:

  • Supplements are not FDA-approved to treat, cure, or prevent diseases
  • Quality and purity vary significantly between brands
  • Interactions with medications are possible
  • Side effects may occur even with "natural" products
  • Long-term safety data is often limited

5. Research Limitations

Studies cited in articles may have limitations:

  • Small sample sizes
  • Short study durations
  • Animal studies (may not translate to humans)
  • Funding bias (industry-funded research)
  • Lack of peer review (preprints)

6. Individual Variation

What works for one person may not work for another. Factors affecting individual response include:

  • Age, genetics, and existing health conditions
  • Current medications and supplements
  • Lifestyle factors (diet, exercise, sleep)
  • Environmental exposures
