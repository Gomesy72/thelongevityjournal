# ConvertKit Welcome Email Automation — Setup Guide

## Step 1: Create New Automation
1. Go to https://app.convertkit.com/automations
2. Click **"New automation"** (top right)
3. Click **"Start from scratch"**

## Step 2: Set the Trigger
1. Click **"Add a trigger"**
2. Select **"Subscribes to a form"**
3. Choose your form: **"Charlotte form"** (Form 9441355)
4. Click **"Add trigger"**

## Step 3: Add Email Action
1. Click the **"+"** icon below the trigger
2. Select **"Email"** → **"Send email"**
3. Click **"Add action"**

## Step 5: Customize the Confirmation Page

The confirmation page is what subscribers see AFTER they click the link in their email. ConvertKit uses a default message, but you can brand it:

1. In your ConvertKit dashboard, go to **Settings** → **Account Settings**
2. Look for **"Confirmation message"** or **"Success page"**
3. Change the text to:

```
Welcome to The Longevity Journal!

Subscription confirmed. You're officially on the list.

Expect your first research briefing this Thursday — plus your free Longevity Protocol PDF.

Here's to living longer and better,
The Longevity Journal Team
```

## Step 6: Set Timing
1. Change the delay from **"1 day"** to **"Immediately"**
2. (Or leave it as "Immediately" — they'll get the email right after confirming)

## Step 7: Publish
1. Click **"Publish"** in the top right
2. Toggle the automation to **"Active"**

## Step 8: Test
1. Go to your website: https://gomesy72.github.io/thelongevityjournal/
2. Subscribe with a test email
3. Confirm the subscription
4. Check your inbox for the welcome email!
